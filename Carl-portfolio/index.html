About

Two skill sets, one analyst.

My background is in finance and accounting practice: bookkeeping, reconciliations, expenditure monitoring and variance reporting against budget. That taught me what accurate numbers look like and why they matter to the people who rely on them.

On top of that I have built the technical side: SQL and PostgreSQL for extracting and structuring data, Power BI for communicating it, Excel for modelling, and Python for analysis. Before that, field research roles on projects connected to UNICEF Kenya and the ILO taught me that data is only as valuable as the decision it enables. I’m now deepening my valuation and financial modelling work with a public-company research project.

How I work

Evidence first, then the opinion.

01

Understand the question

What decision is this for, and who makes it? I start there before I open a single file.

02

Verify the evidence

Primary sources first: audited statements, filings and official data, checked before they are used.

03

Model and quantify

Excel, SQL, Python and Power BI used where they fit the problem, with every assumption written down.

04

Recommend and defend

A clear conclusion, the alternatives I considered, and what could make me wrong.

Four rules I don’t break

Every figure has a source. Assumptions are labelled, never hidden. Limits sit next to the result. A claim stops where the evidence stops.
